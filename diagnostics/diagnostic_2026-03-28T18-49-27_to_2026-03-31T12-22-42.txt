### ANOMALY 2 — hard_stop exits with hold_duration_minutes=0.0 (69 occurrences)
Every hard_stop exit in this window shows hold=0.0 minutes. Pattern:
  - Bot enters position (entry_price=0.005–0.009 for deep OTM NO contracts)
  - Same cycle or next cycle: hard_stop fires with exit_price≈0.50

  remains ≈0.50 (stale). Position enters at live price 0.005, but the hard_stop
  compares against cached mid_price 0.50 — triggering an instant stop at >10x the
  entry price. The pre-GT price refresh (28,328 total) is NOT preventing this because
  these markets' orderbooks are empty/stale post-resolution.

  - KXBRENTD-26MAR3017-T105.50:    20 entries (03-30 10:41 to 14:32), all hard_stop

  - KXBRENTD-26MAR3117-T105.00:     3 entries (03-31 13:22 to 14:35), all hard_stop

### ANOMALY 4 — Grossly inflated PnL on deep-OTM game_final exits

  - KXNCAAWBGAME-26MAR28OKLASCAR:   pnl=$4,945.50   (no entry record in window)
  The PnL formula computes: (size_usd / entry_price) * (1.0 - entry_price), which gives

  would cost $474 in reality but would require a counterparty willing to sell 47k contracts.
  Bankroll ($575.19) reflects these inflated ghost wins.
